Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HiddenRides handles information when you use this website.

Information you provide

HiddenRides currently does not require an account and does not ask visitors to submit their name, phone number or other personal information to browse ride listings. The city search on the site is used to navigate between pages and is not intended to create a user profile.

Technical information

Like most websites, the hosting, security and delivery infrastructure used for HiddenRides may process technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, request time and requested pages for purposes such as security, reliability and service delivery. HiddenRides does not intentionally use this information to build a personal profile.

Cookies and similar technologies

HiddenRides uses Google Analytics 4 (Google tag, Measurement ID G-PKB64MHPTG) to understand website traffic and improve the site. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information such as pages visited, interactions, approximate location derived from IP address, browser/device information and referral details. Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and review Google's privacy information at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Blocking cookies may affect analytics measurement but should not prevent core ride-discovery features.

Google Analytics

Analytics data is used to measure page views and general usage trends, not to ask visitors to create accounts. Do not submit sensitive personal information through the website. Where consent or other privacy requirements apply, analytics should be configured and used accordingly.

External websites

Ride pages may link to services such as Google Maps and image or information sources. When you follow an external link, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply. HiddenRides does not control how third parties process information.

Data protection

Where applicable, HiddenRides aims to handle personal data in accordance with applicable Indian data-protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules as they come into force. Privacy notices and practices may be updated as the website adds features that collect or process personal data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features, technology or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Note: This page is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice.
