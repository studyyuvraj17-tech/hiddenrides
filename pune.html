Rides around Pune

Mountain roads, water and forts within weekend range.

A classic Pune escape through winding Western Ghats roads, green hills and the long Mulshi reservoir.

~45 km*LakeGhats
Pune → Paud → Mulshi

Tamhini Ghat

One of the region's most atmospheric monsoon rides, with forested bends, mist and seasonal waterfalls.

~70 km*ForestWaterfalls
Pune → Mulshi → Tamhini

Lavasa

A winding hill-road escape around the Mulshi region, with lake views, elevated roads and valley scenery.

~60 km*HillsLake

A close-to-city climb with historic fortifications, Sahyadri views and a rewarding mountain approach.

~30 km*FortSunrise

A relaxed reservoir ride through green hills and quiet roads west of Pune, especially rewarding after the monsoon.

~50 km*WaterHills

A longer Sahyadri ride toward one of the region's major hill forts, best paired with a trek.

~60 km*FortTrek
