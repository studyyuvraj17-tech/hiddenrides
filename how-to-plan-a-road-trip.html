05 / A little road wisdom

Small decisions. Better journeys.

The plan should make the ride easier, not turn it into a race against a spreadsheet.

01

Plan by hours, not ego

Traffic, breaks, weather and road quality matter more than a big kilometre target. Leave daylight for unfamiliar stretches.

02

Keep a real buffer

Set aside money for repairs, delays and unexpected stays. Your planned budget is not your emergency fund.

03

Check the road twice

Verify closures, weather, permits and local access shortly before departure, especially on mountain and monsoon routes.

04

Let the route breathe

Leave room for food stops, viewpoints and rest. If you are tired, stop. No destination is worth a rushed ride.

06 / Quick answers

Before you ask the group chat

A few practical answers to the questions that come up on almost every trip.

How do I calculate fuel cost for a group ride?

Divide the total distance by your vehicle's real-world mileage, then multiply by the current fuel price and the number of vehicles. The planner splits that fuel total between the people travelling. For a car, use one vehicle; for a motorcycle group, enter the number of bikes.

How many kilometres should I ride in a day?

There is no universal safe number. Road type, weather, traffic, daylight, terrain, experience and fatigue all matter. Plan by realistic riding hours, include breaks and shorten the day on unfamiliar or mountainous roads.

What should I include in a road trip budget?

Include fuel, accommodation, food, tolls, parking, permits, activities and a contingency buffer. Add vehicle-specific costs such as maintenance or a repair allowance where relevant.

Does this planner show live route distance or fuel prices?

No. You enter the distance, mileage and fuel price. The route templates are illustrative starting points, and the map link opens Google Maps for route checking. Verify current road conditions, prices, permits and access before travelling.

Where is my trip saved?

Choose “Save on this device” to store the trip details and checklist in this browser on this device. It is not an online account or cloud backup. Use “Copy my plan” or “Print / PDF” if you want a portable copy.
