BEFORE YOU SET OFF

Plan the ride.
Ride it right.

Local conditions matter. A little preparation makes a better day on the road.

01 · ROUTE

Confirm the live route

Set your own starting point in Maps. City traffic, roadworks and final permitted access can change distance and journey time.

02 · WEATHER

Choose the right time

Start early in hot weather, carry water and check the forecast. Avoid exposed terrain during extreme heat, storms or poor visibility.

03 · RESPECT

Respect local access

Use public roads and authorised viewpoints. Ask before photographing residents and avoid disturbing wildlife, temples and village life.

GOOD TO KNOW

Jodhpur ride FAQs.

Quick answers to help you plan a safer ride. Always verify current road conditions, opening hours and local access before departure.

Where can I go for a short bike ride near Jodhpur?

Kaylana Lake and nearby countryside can suit a shorter outing. Check local access, traffic and permitted parking before travelling.

Is Osian suitable for a day ride from Jodhpur?

Osian is a possible longer outing from Jodhpur. Check current road conditions and destination timings, and plan to return before dark.

Can I visit Bishnoi villages by motorcycle?

You can plan a route toward the Bishnoi village region, but be respectful of residents and private property. Arrange visits where needed and follow local guidance.

What should I know about riding in desert heat?

Start early, carry water, check tyre condition and fuel, and avoid the hottest part of the day. Do not underestimate distances between services.

What should I carry on a ride around Jodhpur?

Carry water, identification, a charged phone, basic puncture support and sun protection. Check your bike and route before setting off.
