Road routes from Jodhpur

Choose a short scenic loop or plan a longer Rajasthan ride.

Not every good ride needs to be a long highway day. These route ideas combine Jodhpur’s nearby lakes, villages and desert country with longer trips for riders who have more time. Distances and drive times are indicative—check live navigation, local access and weather before setting out.

01 · EASY ESCAPE🌅

Kaylana Lake sunset loop

A relaxed short ride for open skies and an evening stop. Keep to permitted public viewpoints and avoid stopping on the road shoulder.

Short city ride2–3 hours
Jodhpur → Kaylana Lake → Jodhpur
02 · NATURE & VILLAGES🌿

Guda Bishnoi countryside

Ride into rural landscapes known for Bishnoi culture and wildlife. Observe animals quietly, ask before photographing residents and respect private land.

About 22–30 km*Half day
Jodhpur → Bishnoi countryside → Guda
03 · DESERT & HERITAGE🏜️

Osian temple and desert route

A rewarding day ride for historic temples and desert scenery. Plan your temple visit and any dune activity separately, and return with daylight to spare.

About 65 km one way*Half/full day
Jodhpur → Mandore side → Osian
04 · HISTORY & COUNTRYSIDE🌳

Khejarli heritage ride

A quieter rural outing connected with the Bishnoi community’s historic protection of Khejri trees. Keep the visit respectful and low-impact.

About 25 km*Half day
Jodhpur → Khejarli → Jodhpur
05 · VIEWPOINT⛰️

Ghoda Ghati Hills

A short local outing for rocky scenery and a change of pace. Confirm safe public access before visiting and avoid climbing unstable or restricted areas.

Short city ride*2–3 hours
Jodhpur → local hill approach → Jodhpur
06 · LAKE & GREENERY🌳

Balsamand Lake and Mandore Road

A compact ride for a historic reservoir and a greener edge of the city. Check venue access and opening hours before making this the main stop.

About 5 km*1–2 hours
Jodhpur → Mandore Road → Balsamand
07 · CULTURE & LANDSCAPE🌾

Arna Jharna desert museum

A culture-led trip to a desert landscape and museum experience. Confirm visiting arrangements before travelling and allow time for the return.

About 28 km*Half day
Jodhpur → Moklawas → Arna Jharna
08 · LOCAL HIDDEN GEM🛕

Badli–Bheruji rural loop

A quieter outing toward Badli and Bheruji Pond. Check local access and road conditions, and be respectful around the temple and village.

Local ride*Half day
Jodhpur → Badli → Bheruji Pond
Rider checklist: Check tyres, brakes, fuel, weather and road closures; carry water and a charged phone; take breaks; never ride tired. Distances are estimates, not live route calculations.
