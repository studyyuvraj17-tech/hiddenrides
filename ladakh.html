Himalayan motorcycle rides in Ladakh

Rides around Leh & Ladakh

From short Leh viewpoints to multi-day Himalayan journeys.

🏞️High-altitude lake

Pangong Tso

A spectacular high-altitude lake ride across stark Himalayan terrain. The road crosses a high pass, so plan acclimatisation, weather and a daylight return or overnight stay.

~160 km one way*Full day / overnightLake
Leh → Chang La → Pangong Tso
🏔️Mountain pass

Khardung La

A famous high mountain pass north of Leh with dramatic Himalayan views. The ascent is steep and altitude is significant; follow current BRO and local travel advisories.

~40 km one way*Half day, conditions permittingHigh Pass
Leh → South Pullu → Khardung La
🏜️Valley + dunes

Nubra Valley

A multi-day ride through high mountain scenery to a broad valley of rivers, villages and cold-desert dunes. Give yourself time for acclimatisation and avoid rushing the return.

~120 km one way*Overnight / 2 daysValley
Leh → Khardung La → Khalsar → Nubra Valley
🛣️Short scenic ride

Magnetic Hill

A short ride west of Leh through the Indus valley, known for the optical-illusion stopping point and stark mountain scenery. Stop only in safe designated areas away from traffic.

~30 km one way*2–3 hoursLandscape
Leh → NH1 → Magnetic Hill
🩵Remote lake

Tso Moriri

A remote high-altitude lake surrounded by open plateau and mountain landscapes. This is a serious expedition-style ride rather than a casual day trip; plan fuel, permits, lodging and weather carefully.

~220 km one way*2 days / overnightRemote Lake
Leh → Chumathang → Tso Moriri
🌙Monastery + geology

Lamayuru Monastery & Moonland

A westbound ride through the Indus valley to a historic monastery and the unusual eroded formations often called Moonland. Plan a daylight return and allow time for roadside viewpoints.

~115 km one way*Full dayHeritage
Leh → Nimmu → Lamayuru
☸️Leh viewpoint

Shanti Stupa

A convenient local ride or short outing above Leh, with broad views over the town and surrounding mountains. Visit in clear weather and park without blocking access.

Within Leh*1–2 hoursViewpoint
Leh town → Shanti Stupa
⛰️Expedition ride

Zanskar Valley

A remote Himalayan journey toward Padum. The newer Nimmu–Padum route may shorten the trip, but confirm current road status locally; the traditional route via Kargil is much longer.

~190 km via Nimmu–Padum*Multi-day tripExpedition ride
Leh → Nimmu → Padum (verify current road status)
🏔️High mountain pass

Chang La Pass

A dramatic high-altitude pass on the route toward Pangong Tso, with steep gradients and exposed mountain conditions. Do not make this a rushed detour; turn back if weather or road conditions deteriorate.

~75 km one way*Half day, conditions permittingHigh Pass
Leh → Sakti → Chang La
🛕Monastery + viewpoint

Thiksey Monastery

A hilltop monastery east of Leh with layered Ladakhi architecture and wide views over the Indus Valley. Dress respectfully, follow photography rules and check visitor timings before setting out.

~20 km one way*2–3 hoursMonastery
Leh → Indus Valley road → Thiksey
🛕Culture + heritage

Hemis Monastery

A worthwhile ride into a side valley south-east of Leh to one of Ladakh’s best-known Buddhist monasteries. The route pairs well with a slow day exploring the Indus Valley; check opening times and festival dates.

~45 km one way*Half dayMonastery
Leh → Karu → Hemis Monastery
🌿Remote village + culture

Turtuk Village

A long ride beyond Nubra through the Shyok Valley to a village known for its Balti culture, orchards and distinctive local history. Plan an overnight stay, check access and permits, and never rush this route in one day.

~200–210 km one way*Overnight / 2 daysRemote Valley
Leh → Khardung La → Nubra Valley → Turtuk

Choose your ride

Simple ideas for planning around the season.

Plan safely: Leh District advises all tourists to allow at least 48 hours for acclimatisation before travelling to higher-altitude areas. Avoid strenuous activity during this period and do not continue ascending if you feel unwell. Check current road openings, weather, route-specific permits, fuel and accommodation before departure. Distances are approximate one-way estimates and can change with the chosen route. Tso Moriri is a protected wetland: stay on designated public roads and never ride off-road or onto lake shores. Read the official Ladakh travel advisory and check the official Leh tourist permit portal.
