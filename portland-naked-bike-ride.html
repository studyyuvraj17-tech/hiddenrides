Portland Naked Bike Ride

Portland’s World Naked Bike Ride is a community-led celebration and protest that highlights cyclist vulnerability, safer streets, body freedom and the environmental cost of oil-dependent transport.

What the ride stands for

The ride uses visible, people-powered movement to call attention to oil use in transportation, safer cycling infrastructure and the vulnerability of people riding among motor vehicles. It has been part of Portland’s Bike Summer culture since 2004.

What to expect

The organiser describes the event as clothing-optional: participants choose their own comfort level. The 2026 ride gathered at Wilshire Park at 6:30 p.m. and launched at dusk. Future start locations, route and timing should be treated as unconfirmed until organisers publish them.

Plan responsibly

Check the organiser’s latest FAQ for bicycle equipment and safety requirements. Respect other participants’ boundaries and photography preferences, carry lights and water, and check current local rules before attending.
