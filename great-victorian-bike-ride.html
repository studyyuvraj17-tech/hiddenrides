Discover the Victorian Goldfields on two wheels during the 2026 TAC Great Victorian Bike Ride. This five-day edition combines country-road riding with campsite life, local towns, shared meals and a flexible loop-ride format.

About the Great Vic

First held in 1984, the Great Victorian Bike Ride is an annual Bicycle Network event. The 2026 edition is presented as a more accessible five-day Goldfields adventure, with fewer campsite moves, two nights at each campsite and optional loop rides so participants can choose a longer or more relaxed day.

2026 route at a glance

The published host-town sequence is Bendigo, Castlemaine, Trentham and Creswick. The organiser currently lists route options and distances that vary with the selected loop day. One five-day route listing gives 298 km or 343 km, while the general route page advertises a 412 km option. Treat all distances as provisional because the organiser may adjust routes.

What is included?

  • Five days of organised riding and campsite accommodation.
  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner are included as described in the event package, subject to the organiser’s meal schedule.
  • Daily luggage transfers between event campsites.
  • On-route support, rest stops and event crew assistance.
  • Entertainment and activities at the campsites.
  • Flexible loop ride options, with a shorter or longer ride on selected days.

Who can take part?

The event is designed for a range of cycling abilities, with road, hybrid and mountain bikes listed as suitable types. Riders should still prepare for multiple consecutive days in the saddle, check the route distances and select a loop that matches their fitness. Read the organiser’s current entry terms for age, equipment, safety and support requirements.

Plan your ride

  • Build up weekly riding distance before November and practise riding on consecutive days.
  • Check your bike, brakes, tyres, lights and drivetrain before travelling.
  • Pack weather-appropriate layers, rain protection, helmet, repair kit and personal medication.
  • Review the official event guide for campsite facilities, luggage limits, transport and meal arrangements.
  • Follow marshals, road rules and the organiser’s daily route instructions. Do not rely on old GPX files or third-party route maps.
