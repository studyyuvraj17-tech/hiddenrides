Philly Naked Bike Ride

The Philly Naked Bike Ride is an annual community event promoting cycling advocacy, body positivity and fuel-conscious living. It is part of the wider World Naked Bike Ride movement.

What the ride stands for

Organisers describe the ride as a way to support cycling advocacy, positive body image and reduced dependence on fossil fuels. The event has been held annually since 2009, apart from the pandemic interruption.

2026 event notes

The 2026 gathering used the Dragon Boat Dock on Martin Luther King Drive as its meeting point, with the route ending near the Philadelphia Museum of Art. These are historical details for 2026, not confirmed arrangements for the next edition.

Plan responsibly

Check the official blog and social channels for the next date, meeting point and route. Organisers emphasise consent and respectful photography. Bring water, sun protection and a way to carry your belongings.
