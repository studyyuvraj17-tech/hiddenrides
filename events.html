Community ride · Chicago, Illinois

World Naked Bike Ride Chicago

An international, clothing-optional cycling event celebrating human-powered transport, body positivity and freedom from oil. Chicago's ride brings cyclists and skaters together for a large, non-sexual public demonstration.

📍 Chicago, USA🗓️ 2027 date TBA🎟️ Free

Latest edition: Saturday, July 11, 2026. The organisers confirmed that the 2026 ride was a success. Check their official channels for the next date and meeting details.

Why this ride happens

Organizers connect the ride to reducing dependence on oil, making streets safer for cyclists and celebrating body freedom. It is both a protest and a community celebration.

What to expect

It is clothing-optional, not compulsory nudity. The official guidance is to participate only at a level that feels comfortable to you. The ride is community-led and volunteer-run.

Privacy & photography

Organizers discourage photography. Respect participants' privacy, ask for consent before taking or sharing identifiable photos, and put your camera away if asked.

Route & practical planning

The route and finish point are not announced beforehand. The 2026 ride was a point-to-point route of roughly 7–8 miles; check the official FAQ for updated arrangements before attending a future edition.

Planning to attend a future edition?

Use the organizers' own channels for confirmed dates, start details and participation guidance.
