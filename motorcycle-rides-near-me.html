What makes a good motorcycle route?

  • Road surface and access: Check whether the route is paved, under repair, restricted or suitable for your motorcycle.
  • Distance and riding time: Plan fuel, rest and daylight around the full return journey, not only the one-way distance.
  • Season and weather: Monsoon, heat, fog and snow can change road safety and accessibility.
  • Stops along the way: Identify fuel stations, food, water, viewpoints and emergency services before departure.

Ride responsibly

Check current road and weather conditions before leaving. Follow local access rules, wear certified protective gear, carry a valid licence and vehicle documents, and avoid entering restricted, private or environmentally sensitive areas.
