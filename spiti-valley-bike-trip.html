Know before you go

Road, altitude and permit notes

Road status changes quickly. Kunzum Pass is seasonal and can close after snowfall. The district administration notes that the Keylong–Kaza road commonly remains closed in winter, roughly October/November to June/July depending on snow and weather. Confirm same-day status before riding.

Take the Kinnaur approach gradually

The district administration lists Shimla–Kaza at about 412 km. Staging through Narkanda, Rampur, Reckong Peo, Nako and Tabo gives more time to adjust to altitude.

Respect the altitude

Kaza is around 3,800 m and Kunzum Pass around 4,590 m. Avoid rapid ascent. If you develop symptoms of altitude illness, do not ride higher; severe symptoms need urgent medical attention and descent.

Permits

Indian visitors generally do not need an Inner Line Permit for the main Spiti tourist circuit. Foreign nationals may need a permit for restricted border areas on the Kinnaur side. Verify current rules with the district administration.

Fuel, cash and network

Fuel and repairs are limited outside larger settlements. Fill up when practical, carry cash, download offline maps and share your plan. Do not assume mobile data or UPI will work in every village.

Common questions

Spiti Valley bike trip FAQs

How many days are enough?

Allow around 9–12 days for a relaxed circuit from Shimla through Kinnaur and out via Manali, including time around Kaza and a weather buffer.

Which route is better: Shimla or Manali?

Shimla via Kinnaur is the gradual approach. The Manali route via Kunzum is shorter but seasonal and rougher. Combine both for the full circuit when Kunzum is open.

When does Kunzum Pass open?

There is no fixed date. Opening depends on snowfall and clearance. Check current official advisories rather than relying on a previous year's date.

Do Indian citizens need a permit?

Generally no Inner Line Permit is needed for the main Spiti tourist circuit, but restricted border areas can have different rules. Verify before travel.

Is Spiti suitable for a first motorcycle tour?

It is demanding because of altitude, remote stretches, rough surfaces and limited services. Mountain-road experience, a maintained motorcycle and a conservative schedule are strongly recommended.
