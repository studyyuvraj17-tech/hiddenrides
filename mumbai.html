Rides around Mumbai

From quick escapes to full-day Western Ghats rides.

⛰️Ghats

Lonavala & Khandala

The classic Mumbai–Pune escape: winding Ghats roads, viewpoints, valleys and dramatic monsoon scenery.

~65–80 km*HillsMonsoon
Mumbai → Panvel → Lonavala
🌄Hill escape

Matheran

A forested hill destination in the Sahyadris where the final approach gives way to a quieter, vehicle-free hill environment.

~80 km*ForestHills
Mumbai → Karjat → Matheran
🏰Fort

Karnala Fort & Sanctuary

A compact escape into the forested hills near Panvel, combining a historic fort with a wildlife sanctuary.

~55 km*FortForest
Mumbai → Panvel → Karnala
🌊Coast

Alibaug Coastal Ride

Cross the harbour side and ride into coastal Maharashtra for beaches, seafood stops, village roads and sea air.

Day ride*CoastForts
Mumbai → Panvel → Pen → Alibaug
🏝️Sea Fort

Murud–Janjira

A longer Konkan run toward the famous sea fort, with coastal roads and beaches making the journey part of the destination.

~150 km*CoastFull day
Mumbai → Alibaug → Murud
🌧️Monsoon

Malshej Ghat

A longer Western Ghats ride known for dramatic mountain roads, waterfalls and mist during the monsoon season.

~130 km*GhatsWaterfalls
Mumbai → Murbad → Malshej
🌲Forest

Bhimashankar

A forested Sahyadri destination with winding approach roads, dense greenery and a strong monsoon character.

~210 km*ForestWeekend
Mumbai → Karjat → Bhimashankar
⛰️Fort + Trek

Rajmachi

A Sahyadri adventure around the historic Rajmachi forts, best approached as a longer ride combined with a trek.

~80–100 km*FortTrek
Mumbai → Lonavala → Rajmachi
