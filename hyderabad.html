Rides around Hyderabad

From quick lake loops to full-day countryside rides.

🌄📍 Ananthagiri Hills, VikarabadHills + Forest

Ananthagiri Hills

A popular green escape west of Hyderabad, with forested roads, hill scenery and the Anantha Padmanabha Swamy Temple nearby.

~80 km*Half / full dayHills
Hyderabad → Vikarabad → Ananthagiri Hills
🏰📍 Bhongir Fort, TelanganaFort + Heritage

Bhongir Fort

Ride east to the dramatic hilltop fort set on a distinctive rocky outcrop. The final visit involves walking uphill, so plan footwear and time.

~50–60 km*Half dayFort
Hyderabad → Bhongir → Bhongir Fort
🌊📍 Osman Sagar, HyderabadLake + Sunset

Osman Sagar (Gandipet)

A short city-edge ride toward the reservoir landscape at Gandipet, ideal when you want open skies without committing to a full-day trip.

~25 km*2–3 hoursLake
Hyderabad → Gandipet → Osman Sagar
🪨📍 Rachakonda Fort, TelanganaRocky Trails + History

Rachakonda Fort

A rugged ride toward a historic fort landscape surrounded by rocky hills and open countryside. Expect a walk to explore the fort area.

~60–70 km*Half dayRocky Trails
Hyderabad → Rachakonda → Fort approach
💧📍 Singur Dam, TelanganaReservoir + Countryside

Singur Dam

A longer westward ride through the countryside toward the Singur reservoir. The journey is as much about open roads as the destination.

~70–80 km*Half / full dayReservoir
Hyderabad → Sangareddy side → Singur Dam
🏯📍 Medak Fort, TelanganaHeritage Day Ride

Medak Fort

A longer heritage ride to Medak’s hilltop fort, with town stops and regional history along the way.

~95–100 km*Full dayHeritage Day Ride
Hyderabad → Medak → Medak Fort
🎬📍 Ramoji Film City, HyderabadCity Edge + Experience

Ramoji Film City

A straightforward ride to one of the region’s best-known visitor attractions. This is a ticketed destination, so plan around entry times and parking rules.

~30–40 km*Half / full dayCity Edge
Hyderabad → Abdullapurmet → Ramoji Film City
🛣️📍 Nalgonda, TelanganaHighway + Countryside

Nalgonda Countryside Ride

A longer southeast ride through highway and countryside stretches, suited to riders who want a full-day plan beyond the city limits.

~100–110 km*Full dayHighway
Hyderabad → Choutuppal → Nalgonda
⛰️📍 Chityal, TelanganaRocky Hills + Open Roads

Chityal Hills

A ride idea for exploring the rocky countryside around Chityal. Treat informal viewpoints as unverified until access and safe parking are confirmed.

~80–100 km*Half / full dayRocky Hills
Hyderabad → Chityal → local hill approaches
🌳📍 Narsapur Forest, TelanganaForest + Green Roads

Narsapur Forests

A green escape northwest of the city, with wooded surroundings and quieter roads near Narsapur. Keep the ride low-impact and daylight-only.

~45–55 km*Half dayForest
Hyderabad → Medchal → Narsapur Forest
