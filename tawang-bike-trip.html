Know before you go

Road, altitude and permit notes

Mountain conditions can change in hours. Heavy rain, landslides, snowfall and temporary security restrictions can affect the route. Keep a buffer day, avoid night riding and confirm local road status before leaving each halt.

Get your Arunachal ILP

Indian nationals need an Inner Line Permit to enter Arunachal Pradesh. Apply through the official eILP portal before reaching the Bhalukpong check gate. Carry the permit and the ID used in the application.

Acclimatise before the high pass

Tawang is around 3,000 m and Sela Pass is about 4,170 m. Stay overnight at Bomdila or Dirang and take it easy on arrival. Head down and seek medical help if altitude symptoms worsen.

Bum La needs extra clearance

The Bum La border area is not covered by the state ILP. Indian visitors need separate local/Army clearance arranged in Tawang; access and vehicle rules can change. Confirm the current process with the district administration and use an authorised local vehicle where required.

Fuel, cash and network

Refuel when practical, carry cash, download offline maps and keep your phone charged. Mobile coverage and digital payments can be unreliable on remote stretches and near high passes.

Respect local culture and nature

Tawang is home to Monpa communities and important Buddhist sites. Dress respectfully at monasteries, follow photography instructions, take all waste back and never walk onto frozen lakes.

Choose the right season

March–June and September–November are generally the more practical windows. July–August brings monsoon rain and landslide risk; December–February can bring severe cold, snow and closures on exposed roads.

Common questions

Tawang bike trip FAQs

How many days are enough for a Tawang bike trip?

Allow 6–8 days from Guwahati, including an overnight halt at Dirang or Bomdila each way, a full day in Tawang and at least one weather buffer. Add another day for Bum La and Sangetsar Tso if conditions allow.

Do Indian citizens need a permit for Tawang?

Yes. Indian nationals from outside Arunachal Pradesh need an Inner Line Permit (ILP) to enter the state. Bum La and certain border areas require additional clearance.

What is the best time to ride to Tawang?

March to June and September to November are generally the best windows. Avoid planning a tight schedule during the July–August monsoon. Winter rides require experience, suitable equipment and current confirmation that roads are open.

Is Sela Pass open throughout the year?

The Sela Tunnel provides an all-weather road connection, but weather, snow and road conditions can still disrupt travel. The old Sela Pass road and lake viewpoint may be inaccessible during bad weather. Check locally before riding.

Can I ride my own motorcycle to Bum La?

Do not assume that your own motorcycle can be taken to Bum La. Border-area access is controlled and vehicle rules can change. Confirm the latest instructions with the Tawang district administration; authorised local transport may be required.

Is the Tawang route suitable for a beginner?

It is a demanding mountain ride with long days, steep gradients, changing weather and limited services. Riders should be comfortable with mountain roads, have a well-maintained motorcycle and avoid riding after dark.
