London to Brighton Bike Ride

The iconic London to Brighton Cycle Ride takes riders from Clapham Common through the Surrey and Sussex countryside to Brighton seafront. The 2026 edition took place on 13 September; the next Skyline-organised ride is scheduled for Sunday 12 September 2027.

About the ride

This is a supported mass-participation road cycling challenge open to riders of different experience levels. Participants can ride independently, with a group or in support of a charity. The organiser provides a signed route, event crew, medical support, water stops and mechanical assistance.

Route and key challenge

The ride starts at Clapham Common in south London and finishes on Madeira Drive on Brighton seafront. The route passes through the southern London suburbs and countryside in Surrey and Sussex. The best-known climb is Ditchling Beacon in the South Downs. The course is predominantly on public roads, so riders should be confident cycling in traffic and prepare in advance.

2026 edition and next date

The 2026 Skyline ride was held on Sunday 13 September 2026. The organiser’s current website lists the next edition for Sunday 12 September 2027, with entry advertised at £55. Confirm prices, availability, start waves and terms directly with the organiser before booking.

Preparation and safety

  • Build up to longer road rides before event day and practise climbing.
  • Service your bicycle and carry a spare inner tube, pump and basic repair kit.
  • The organiser requires a correctly fitted cycle helmet and a roadworthy bicycle with functioning front and rear brakes.
  • Follow event arrows, marshals and road instructions; carry water and use the designated support stops.
  • Check the organiser’s rules for permitted bicycle types, accessibility arrangements and return transport.

HiddenRides is an independent ride guide, not the event organiser. “London to Brighton” is also used for other rides organised by different charities and groups. This page covers the Skyline London to Brighton Cycle Ride; always check the organiser and date before registering.
