A little beyond the city

A simple ride. A different pace.

Badli Bheruji Pond is a local waterbody and rural stop outside Jodhpur. Water levels and bird activity vary by season, so treat it as a peaceful countryside outing rather than a guaranteed wildlife sighting.

Take the ride at an easy pace, check the route before leaving, and enjoy the change from city streets to open countryside.

Route note: Distance and time are estimates, not live-tested motorcycle timings. Confirm the exact route, access and road conditions in Maps before departure.

01 / On the road

Road experience

A rural ride from Jodhpur toward Badli. Confirm the current route and road conditions before leaving, and allow extra time for slower stretches, traffic and breaks.

02 / At the pond

What to see and do

Enjoy the landscape from public roads and permitted viewpoints. Observe birds from a distance and do not enter fields or private land without permission.

03 / Before you go

Practical preparation

Refuel and carry water in Jodhpur before departure; facilities near the pond may be limited. Save your return route and confirm local access conditions.

04 / Ride responsibly

Respect the place

  • Observe birds without feeding or disturbing them.
  • Respect residents, farmland and access restrictions.
  • Avoid blocking rural roads or stopping in live traffic for photos.
Before you set off

Ride safe. Leave no trace.

A little preparation makes a short ride more enjoyable—for you and the people who live along the route.

  • Check weather, daylight and current road/access conditions.
  • Wear a certified helmet and protective riding gear.
  • Avoid speeding and risky overtakes.
  • Carry water, a charged phone and emergency contacts.
  • Respect wildlife, local residents and private property.
  • Refuel before leaving the city if needed.
