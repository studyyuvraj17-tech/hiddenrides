Why ride to Jhanwar?

Jhanwar Road is a simple countryside escape for riders who want a break from city traffic and a slower view of rural life west of Jodhpur. Expect open farmland, village roads and a relaxed outing rather than a packed itinerary.

Plan your route

Use Jodhpur as your starting point and confirm the exact route in live navigation before leaving. The distance depends on where in the city you start and the road you choose.

  • Fuel up in the city before heading out.
  • Ride cautiously through village areas and around livestock.
  • Carry water and avoid stopping on narrow or busy road shoulders.

Ride responsibly
