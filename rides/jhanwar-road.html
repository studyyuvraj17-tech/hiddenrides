Jhanwar Road

A quieter ride west of Jodhpur through the Jhanwar countryside, with open farmland, green fields and a relaxed rural Rajasthani landscape.

Why make this ride?

Jhanwar Road is a simple countryside escape for riders who want a break from city traffic and a slower view of rural life west of Jodhpur.

  • Open farmland and rural Rajasthani scenery.
  • A short outing that can fit into a relaxed morning.
  • Good opportunity to explore the roads around Jhanwar without treating the route as a timed itinerary.

Route at a glance

Use Jodhpur as your starting point and confirm the exact route in live navigation before leaving. The distance depends on where in the city you start and the road you choose.

  • Fuel up in the city before heading into rural stretches.
  • Carry water, a charged phone and proper riding gear.
  • Use public roads only and respect local residents and private property.
