💦 Beri Ganga Waterfall

Beri Ganga is a small, rainfall-dependent cascade near Beri Ganga Mahadev Mandir in the Mandore area of Jodhpur. It is not a year-round waterfall: flow may be strongest during or shortly after monsoon rain and weak or absent in dry periods. Check recent local conditions before making the ride specifically for the waterfall.

Why ride here?

Beri Ganga is a local nature and religious stop near Mandore, beside Beri Ganga Mahadev Mandir. Rainfall determines whether the seasonal cascade is flowing, so treat the waterfall as a weather-dependent destination rather than a guaranteed attraction.

Location and bike route

The destination is in the Mandore side of Jodhpur, beside Beri Ganga Mahadev Mandir. From Paota or central Jodhpur, plan a route toward Mandore and confirm the final approach locally. The approximate 20 km figure is a planning estimate, not a fixed verified distance; your actual distance will vary with your starting point and access.

Road and terrain

The approach is a local ride toward Mandore, followed by access around a natural rocky site. Expect uneven ground near the cascade. After rain, mud, loose stones and wet rock can make the final walk slippery; do not ride onto footpaths or leave public roads without permission.

What to see and do

Visit Beri Ganga Mahadev Mandir respectfully and view the seasonal cascade from safe, publicly accessible ground. Water flow is weather-dependent, so a visit in the dry season may not include a visible waterfall. Do not enter the water, climb wet rock faces or approach unstable edges.

Best time and nearby stops

Visit in daylight during or soon after monsoon rain if your priority is seeing water flow. Avoid heavy rain, thunderstorms and unsafe weather. Mandore is the nearest wider area for planning food, fuel and other stops; check access rules of any additional attraction separately. Browse the Jodhpur ride guide for more local destinations.

Practical stops

Identify a fuel station and a food or restroom stop near Mandore before leaving; facilities close to the waterfall may be limited. Check access and local conditions before setting out.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Beri Ganga Waterfall?

It is near Beri Ganga Mahadev Mandir in the Mandore area of Jodhpur, Rajasthan.

Does the waterfall flow throughout the year?

No. It is seasonal and depends on rainfall; flow may be weak or absent in dry weather.

When should I visit?

Daylight during or shortly after monsoon rain offers a better chance of seeing water, but avoid heavy rain or unsafe weather.

Is it safe to climb near the waterfall?

Wet rocks and uneven ground can be slippery. Stay on safe public paths, keep away from edges and do not enter the water.
