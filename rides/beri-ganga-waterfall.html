💦 Beri Ganga Waterfall

A small seasonal cascade near Beri Ganga Mahadev Mandir in the Mandore area of Jodhpur. It is most photogenic during and just after the monsoon; flow can be weak or absent in dry weather.

Why ride here?

Beri Ganga is a local nature and religious stop near Mandore, beside Beri Ganga Mahadev Mandir. Rainfall determines whether the seasonal cascade is flowing, so treat the waterfall as a weather-dependent destination rather than a guaranteed attraction.

Road experience

A short ride from Jodhpur toward Mandore, with a nature stop at the end. Treat the distance as an estimate, check the route preview before leaving, and allow extra time for traffic, breaks and stops.

What to see and do

Visit the temple and enjoy the scenery only from publicly accessible areas. After rain, rocks and paths can be slippery; avoid climbing beside the cascade or entering fast-moving water.

Practical stops

Identify a fuel station and a food or restroom stop near Mandore before leaving; facilities close to the waterfall may be limited. Check access and local conditions before setting out.

  • Open the map and confirm the current route from your starting point.
  • Carry water, and save a return-route option before departure.
  • Respect temple visitors, local residents and any access restrictions.

Safety checklist

  • Check weather, daylight and current road/access conditions.
  • Wear a certified helmet and protective gear; ride within your ability.
  • Avoid speeding, risky overtakes and stopping in live traffic for photos.
  • Do not climb wet rocks or enter the waterfall pool or flowing water.
  • Carry a charged phone and basic emergency contacts.
