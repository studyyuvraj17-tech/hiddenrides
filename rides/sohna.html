Sohna & Aravalli Roads Bike Ride

Head south from Gurugram toward Sohna for a change from the city grid: rural stretches, village landscapes and views of the low Aravalli hills. Treat this as a flexible half-day ride idea rather than a promise of empty roads; traffic and access vary by route and time.

Suggested routeDelhi NCR → Gurugram → Sohna
Approximate distanceAbout 45–60 km one way, depending on your starting point*
Suggested timingHalf day · Early morning is usually more comfortable

What to expect

The Sohna side of the Delhi NCR offers a nearby countryside escape toward the Aravalli landscape. The appeal is the transition from busy urban roads to more open stretches and village scenery. Choose your turnaround point based on current road conditions and the time you have available.

Route planning and practical tips

Use the route as a starting point, not live navigation instructions. Confirm the current road route, traffic, weather and any access restrictions before departure. Distances are estimates and will vary with your exact starting point and stops.

  • Start early to reduce exposure to heavier traffic and midday heat.
  • Fuel up before leaving built-up areas and carry water and a charged phone.
  • Use public roads only; do not enter private land, fenced areas or restricted tracks.
  • Wear a helmet and protective riding gear, and avoid stopping on blind bends or unsafe shoulders.
  • Respect village residents, local traffic and wildlife; keep noise low in rural stretches.
