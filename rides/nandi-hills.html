Why ride to Nandi Hills?

Karnataka Tourism describes Nandi Hills as a scenic historic retreat about 60 km from Bengaluru and a popular destination for bikers and cyclists.

Route planning and practical tips

Use the route above as a starting point, not a live navigation instruction. Confirm the current road route, access restrictions, weather, opening hours and parking before departure. Distance and travel time can vary with your starting point, traffic and stops.

  • Check fuel availability and plan a break before rural or hilly stretches.
  • Carry water, a charged phone and appropriate riding gear.
  • Use only publicly accessible roads and viewpoints; respect local residents and restricted areas.
  • Avoid stopping in live traffic or taking photographs from unsafe road shoulders.
Accuracy note: Distances and timings are approximate planning guidance. Verify current conditions locally before setting out.
