Rides around Delhi NCR

From quick escapes to full-day rides.

🌊Lake

Damdama Lake

A classic south-NCR escape near Sohna with lake views, birdlife and outdoor activities. Haryana Tourism lists boating, kayaking and trekking here.

~50 km*WaterNature
Delhi → Gurugram → Sohna → Damdama
🦅Birds

Sultanpur Bird Sanctuary

A quieter western-NCR destination for wetlands, birding and open landscapes just outside Gurugram.

~45 km*BirdsNature
Delhi → Dwarka → Gurugram → Sultanpur
🌿Aravalli

Sohna & Aravalli Roads

Head south from Gurugram toward the Aravallis for quieter stretches, village landscapes and a change from the city grid.

~45 km*AravalliRural
Delhi NCR → Gurugram → Sohna
🏰Heritage

Neemrana Fort Ride

A longer weekend run south-west from NCR, combining highway riding with a historic fort destination in Rajasthan.

~120 km*HeritageDay ride
Delhi NCR → NH48 → Neemrana
⛰️Ridge

Asola–Bhatti Landscape

Southern Delhi's rocky ridge and scrub landscape offers a very different riding backdrop without leaving the NCR.

Short ride*RockyNature
Delhi → Chhatarpur → Asola
🛣️Loop

Damdama–Sohna Loop

A route concept connecting the lake and Sohna side roads for riders who want a loop rather than an out-and-back.

Half day*LoopAravalli
Gurugram → Damdama → Sohna → NCR
🌄Escape

Deva–Aravalli Countryside

A quieter rural ride concept toward the south-western edge of NCR, built around open roads and Aravalli scenery.

Day ride*RuralHills
NCR → Aravalli belt → Deva
⛰️Weekend

Morni Hills

A proper weekend ride into Haryana's Shivalik hills, with forested slopes, lakes and mountain roads. Farther than a day-to-day NCR escape.

~250 km*HillsWeekend
Delhi NCR → Panipat → Panchkula → Morni
💧Hidden Lake

Death Valley, Faridabad

An offbeat Aravalli lake landscape near Asola–Faridabad, known for its striking blue water and rugged former-mining terrain. Do not enter the water.

Faridabad*LakeAravalli
Delhi → Surajkund → Asola → Death Valley
