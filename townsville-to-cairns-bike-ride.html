Townsville to Cairns Bike Ride

The Townsville to Cairns Bike Ride is a community-supported, multi-day cycling challenge across North Queensland. Riders travel north from Townsville to Cairns while raising funds for childhood cancer research through Children’s Cancer Institute.

About the Townsville to Cairns Bike Ride

Founded in 1999, the Townsville to Cairns Bike Ride brings cyclists, volunteers and support crews together for a fundraising ride benefiting research into childhood cancer. The event has raised millions of dollars for Children’s Cancer Institute and continues to build community around the cause.

Route at a glance

The ride follows a northbound route through North Queensland, with overnight stops and organised support along the way. The organiser’s published 2026 course covered 374 km over three days, including Townsville to Ingham, Ingham to Innisfail and Innisfail to Cairns. Check the 2027 course guide for confirmed distances, stops and route changes.

Who can participate?

The event welcomes riders with the fitness, group-riding experience and enthusiasm needed for a multi-day road ride. Participants should review the official entry conditions, fundraising requirements, safety rules and support arrangements before registering.

Plan your ride

  • Build up your cycling distance and practise riding on consecutive days.
  • Check your bike, tyres, brakes, drivetrain and helmet before the event.
  • Review the organiser’s current route, daily schedule and equipment requirements.
  • Prepare for North Queensland weather and carry suitable hydration and sun protection.
  • Follow event leaders, support crews, traffic laws and the official course instructions.
