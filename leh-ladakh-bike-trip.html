Before you leave

Leh Ladakh bike trip FAQs

How long should I plan for a Leh Ladakh bike trip?

Allow around 8–12 days for an approach route and local rides, with additional buffer days if possible. The exact duration depends on whether you travel via Manali or Srinagar and which valleys and lakes you include.

How much time should I spend acclimatising in Leh?

Allow at least 48 hours in Leh before travelling to higher-altitude areas. Keep activity light, avoid alcohol and do not continue ascending if you feel unwell. Headache, nausea, breathlessness at rest or confusion need urgent medical attention and descent.

Manali–Leh or Srinagar–Leh: which route should I choose?

Manali–Leh is higher and more remote, with several passes above 4,800 m and long stretches with limited fuel. Srinagar–Leh climbs more gradually via Sonamarg and Kargil and is generally a gentler approach for acclimatisation.

Do I need permits for Ladakh?

Some areas, including Nubra Valley and Pangong, require permits or applicable fees. Requirements can change. Check the official Leh permit portal and carry required documents and ID.

Can I rent a bike in Leh?

Bike rentals are available in Leh. Inspect tyres, brakes, chain, lights and documents before accepting a motorcycle. Confirm local restrictions for your intended route and take photos of any existing damage.

What should I do if I experience symptoms of altitude sickness?

Do not ride higher. Stop, seek medical help and descend if symptoms occur. Severe breathlessness, confusion, loss of coordination or worsening symptoms are emergencies. Speak to a doctor before travel about personal medical risks and preventive options.
