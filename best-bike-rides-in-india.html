Plan a safer ride

  • Check local road closures, weather and seasonal access shortly before departure.
  • Choose a route that suits your motorcycle, experience and riding group.
  • Carry protective gear, water, a first-aid kit, valid licence and vehicle documents.
  • Respect local communities, wildlife, private property and protected areas.

Ride responsibly

Check current road and weather conditions before leaving. Follow local access rules, wear certified protective gear, carry a valid licence and vehicle documents, and avoid entering restricted, private or environmentally sensitive areas.
