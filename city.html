Rides around Jodhpur

🌿Nature

Guda Bishnoi Lake & Villages

Head into the Bishnoi countryside for village landscapes, wildlife and the Guda waterbody. Rajasthan Tourism notes migratory birds, antelopes and blackbucks in the area.

~22–30 km*Half dayWildlife
Jodhpur → Bishnoi countryside → Guda
🏘️Village

Khejarli Village

A rural ride with a powerful environmental history. Khejarli is associated with the 1730 sacrifice of Bishnoi villagers protecting Khejri trees.

SouthwestHistoryRural roads
Jodhpur → Khejarli
🏜️Desert

Osian Desert Ride

A classic longer ride from Jodhpur into the Thar edge: ancient temples, open desert countryside and dunes that become especially atmospheric around sunset.

~65 km one way*Day rideSunset
Jodhpur → Mathania → Osian

Arna Jharna

A desert museum and landscape experience at Moklawas, surrounded by rocky plains, protected forest areas, sacred spots and water bodies.

~28 km*CultureNature
Jodhpur → Moklawas → Arna Jharna

Short rideLakeSunset
